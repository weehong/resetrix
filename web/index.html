01What does Resetrix build?

We design and engineer custom web and mobile applications, backend systems, integrations, and cloud foundations. We can support the full journey from product strategy and UX through deployment and ongoing improvement.

02Why choose custom software over SaaS?

SaaS is a strong fit when standard workflows are enough. Custom software becomes valuable when your process, customer experience, integrations, or growth plans create requirements a generic product cannot serve well.

03What if requirements change?

Change is expected. Short delivery cycles, regular demonstrations, and explicit decisions let us assess new information early and adjust the plan without losing sight of the intended outcome.

04How do you choose the technology?

We start with product goals, users, security, integrations, operating cost, and the skills needed to maintain the system. The stack follows those constraints, not the other way around.

05What happens after launch?

We can continue with monitoring, maintenance, security updates, technical guidance, and iterative product improvements so the software keeps pace with the business.
